Regex Validation Specifications
  • Name: ^[a-zA-Z\s]{3,}$ (Alphabets and spaces only)
  • Email: ^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$ (Standard email structure)
  • Phone: ^[6-9]\d{9}$ (10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9)
  • Password: ^(?=.*[a-z])(?=.*[A-Z])(?=.*\d)(?=.*[@$!%*?&])[A-Za-z\d@$!%*?&]{8,}$ (Upper, lower, digit & symbol)
  • Department: Must select a valid department from dropdown list
